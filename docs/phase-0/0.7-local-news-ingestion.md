# Phase 0.7 — Local News Ingestion

## Purpose
Prove that Local Scoop can ingest authoritative local-government updates and professional local reporting, determine POC-market relevance, classify stories into categories plus searchable tags, isolate source failures, and conservatively cluster overlapping coverage without erasing publisher provenance.

## Tag/search model
Phase 0.7 formalizes a shared controlled tag registry. Category, subcategory, and tag are separate indexed dimensions. Event taxonomy moves to `tax2` and exposes tags in addition to its existing category/subcategory/attribute output.

## Initial news sources
Enabled POC sources are City of Raleigh News, City of Durham NewsFlash, Chatham County News, Johnston County Public Information Office, and WRAL Local News RSS. Wake County official news and the News & Observer local section remain catalogued candidates until their preferred ingestion/terms paths are validated.

## Dynamic catalog
News uses the Phase 0.5 SourceCatalog. `content_types` allows one server-managed catalog to distinguish event and news sources while preserving the same enable/disable/trust/lifecycle controls. User-proposed RSS/Atom feeds may be marked as news, remain Tier 4/proposed, and require approval before ingestion.

## Story model
A `story1:` candidate preserves publisher identity, external ID, headline, publication time, URL, geography, category, tags, retrieval time, raw evidence hash, and local-relevance score.

## Geography and relevance
Stories inherit a single-county authoritative source scope when appropriate and can infer POC counties from locality terms. Broad professional-media feeds must show POC-market geography before inclusion.

## Clustering
Stories are not destructively deduplicated. Similar coverage published within a bounded window can enter a `newscluster1:` cluster. Each original story remains available. Clustering uses headline similarity, tags, geography, and publication proximity and intentionally avoids unlimited transitive merging.

## Source health
Each source reports fetched/accepted/rejected/non-local counts and independent errors. One failed publisher cannot suppress the remaining news feed.

## Deferred/future sources
The maintained backlog includes additional local media, schools, police/fire/public-safety releases, state transportation, business media, chambers/economic-development sources, and other county/municipal publishers.

## Phase 0.8 handoff
Use canonical events, clustered news, weather, alerts, geography, provenance and tag/search metadata as the grounded input set for the Daily Scoop AI synthesis POC.
